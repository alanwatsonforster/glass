# Ground Units

## Design Language

The design language for ground units combines:

- an optional mobility or protection symbol
- an optional role symbol
- an optional weight-class symbol
- an option headquarters symbol
- air-capability text or unit size symbol

The symbols are based on NATO Joint Military Symbology, but with adaptations.

### Mobility or Protection Symbols

The mobility or protection symbols can be:
- Armored: The unit is vehicular, and the vehicles provide armored protection for their crews and loads.
- mobile: the unit is vehicular, but the vehicles do not provide armored protection for their crews and loads.
- Infantry: the unit is not vehicular and moves on foot.
- Absent: the unit is towed.

Both armor and mobile units may be wheeled, half-tracked, or tracked; the distinction is by protection. So, for example, an unarmored tracked vehicle such as the M548 is classified as a mobile unit.

### Equipment Symbols

The role symbols can be:
- Gun: The unit is equipped with direct-fire guns, for example, tanks or IFVs.
- AAA: The unit is equipped with AAA.
- SAM: The unit is equipped with SAMs.
- Radar: The unit is equipped with an EWR or AAA FCR.
- Air Defense: The unit is equipped not equipped with any of the previous systems, but nevertheless its principal role is air defense.
- Artillery: The unit is equipped with indirect fire guns.
- Rocket Artillery: The unit is equipped with indirect fire rockets.
- Missile Artillery: The unit is equipped with indirect fire missiles.
- Absent: The unit has no special equipment and serves to transport or tow other units.

### Weight Class

The letter H, M, or L: These are only used with armored or mobile in units and indicate heavy, medium, and light vehicles.

### Headquarters Symbol

The headquarters symbol denotes a headquarters unit.

### Air-Capability Text or Symbol

The air-capability text or symbol can be:
- Text describing the AAA or SAM system, e.g., “23×4” or “Rapier”.
- Text describing the radar system, e.g., “EWR-A” or “FCR-B”.
- The text “CCU” denoting an air defense command and control unit.
- Symbols for battery, platoon, or team.

## Mounted and Towed Units

An infantry unit may be associated with a transport unit (e.g., a truck, APC, or IFV unit). A towed unit may be associated with a tractor unit (e.g., a truck unit). For example, a mechanized infantry platoon might consist of an infantry platoon and a light APC platoon.
