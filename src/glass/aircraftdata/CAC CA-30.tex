\aircrafttype{CAC CA-30}

The Commonwealth Aircraft Corporation (CAC) CA-30 is a jet trainer with a secondary light attack capability. It is a license-built version of the Aermacchi MB-326H. In RAAF service, it is informally known as the “Macchi”.

\subsection{Version}

The CAC CA-30 is the only version.

It served in the Australian RAAF and RAN from 1967 to 2001. It replaced the DHA Vampire T.33A, T.34A, and T.35 trainers.

\subsection{Armament and Stores}

The CA-30 has no internal guns, but can mount two SUU-11A/A 7.62 mm gun pods with a rate of fire of 2000 rpm per pod and 200 rounds per pod (3 ammo). It can also carry eight 25 kg practice bombs or, presumably in an emergency situation, 250-lb or 500-lb bombs. To increase its range or endurance, it may use 300L fuel tanks.
