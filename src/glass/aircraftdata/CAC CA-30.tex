\aircrafttype{CAC CA-30}

The Commonwealth Aircraft Corporation (CAC) CA-30 is a jet trainer with a secondary light attack capability. It is a license-built version of the Aermacchi MB-326H and served in the Australian RAAF and RAN from 1967 to 2001.
