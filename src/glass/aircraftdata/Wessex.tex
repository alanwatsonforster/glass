\subsection{Westland Wessex}

The Westland Wessex is a medium anti-submarine, transport, and utility helicopter. It is a licensed version of the Sikorsky S-58 with the radial piston engine replaced by one or two tuboshaft engines.

The HAS.1 and HAS.3 versions are anti-submarine versions. They can be equipped with either a dipping sonar or with torpedos or depth charges, so tend to operate in hunter-killer pairs. The HC.2 and HU.5 are transport versions.

The HAS.1/HAS.3/HU.5 served in the Royal Navy from 1961 until about 1990. The HC.2 served in the Royal Air Force from 1962 to 2022.
