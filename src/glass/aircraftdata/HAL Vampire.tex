\aircrafttype{HAL Vampire}

The Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL) Vampire FB.52 is a day fighter and fighter-bomber. It is a license-built version of the de Havilland Vampire FB.52 and T.55. 

HAL built a total of two hundred and forty-seven FB.52s and sixy T.55s aircraft for the Indian IAF AMI with deliveries starting inFebruary 1952.
