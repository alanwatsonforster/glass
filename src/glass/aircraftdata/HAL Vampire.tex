\section{HAL Vampire}

The Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (HAL) Vampire FB.52 is a day fighter and fighter-bomber.

\subsection{Versions}

\subsubsection{HAL Vampire FB.52}

The HAL Vampire FB.52 day fighter and fighter-bomber is license-built version of the de Havilland Vampire FB.52.

It served in the Indian IAF from 1952 but was relegated to a training role after 1971. HAL built a total of two hundred and forty-seven.

\subsubsection{HAL Vampire T.55}

The HAL Vampire T.55 trainer is license-built version of the de Havilland Vampire T.55. A small number were adapted in 1959 for photo-reconnaissance and designated PR.55.

It served in the Indian IAF from 1952 to 1989. HAL built a total of sixty.


\subsection{Armament and Stores}

The gun armament is four Hispano 20 mm cannons with 150 rounds per gun. 

A typical air-to-air load would be two 450L (100 imperial gallon) fuel tanks to increase endurance. 

A typical air-to-ground load would be eight RP-3 rockets and then either two 500 lb bombs or fuel tanks, depending on the mission radius. On short-range missions, two 1,000-lb bombs could be carried but without rockets.
