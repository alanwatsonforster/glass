\aircrafttype{SNCASE Vampire}

The SNCASE (Societé nationale des constructions aéronautiques du Sud-Est) Vampire FB.5 is a day fighter and fighter bomber. It is a license-built version of the de Havilland Vampire FB.5.

It served in the French AA, principally as a day fighter, from 1950 until at least 1954.

Its gun armament is four 20 mm Hispano cannons with 150 rounds, as in the Vampire. A typical air-to-air load would be two 450L fuel tanks to increase endurance.
