# Macchi Vampire

## Notes

Macchi built FB.52A with deliveries starting in December 1951 (Watkins).

The license agreement was for the Goblin 2 (Watkins), confirming that the 52A indeed used this engine and not the Goblin 3.

Production (Watkins):
- First block: 27 
- Second block: 17
- Third batch: 45
- Total: 89

## Bibliography
