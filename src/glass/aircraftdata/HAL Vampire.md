# HAL Vampire

## Notes and Variants

## FB.52

- 247 built under license by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) at Bangalore (Watkins)
- First flew in February 1952 (Watkins)

## T.55

- 60 built under license by Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL) at Bangalore (Watkins)
- First flew in February 1952 (Watkins)

### PR.55

- T.55 adapted with cameras for photo-reconnaissance (Watkins)
- First entered service in 1954 (Watkins)
- Served in 1965 war (Watkins)
- Not clear if it retained its gun armament
