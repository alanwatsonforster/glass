\aircrafttype{Embraer AT-26 Xavante}

The Embraer AT-26 Xavante is a trainer and light attacjk aircraft.

The AT-26 is a two-seat trainer with a secondary light attack capability. It is a license-built version of the Aermacchi MB-326GC. The manufacturer’s designation is EMB-326. The RT-26 is a reconnaissance version of the AT-26

The AT-26A is a single-seat light attack aircraft. It is a rebuilt ex-SAAF Impala Mk II. 

The AT-26 and AT-26A served with the Brazilian FAB from 1971 to 2010 and from 2004 to 2009, respectively. The AY-26 also served  with the Argentinian naval COAN after 1982, making up for MB-326G aircraft lost in the South Atlantic War. 

Armament options include 250-lb and 500-lb bombs, 7.62 mm gun pods, and rocket pods with seven, nineteen, or thirty-seven 70 mm rockets.
