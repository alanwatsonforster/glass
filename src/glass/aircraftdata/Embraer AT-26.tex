\aircrafttype{Embraer AT-26/RT-26 Xavante}

The Embraer AT-26 Xavante is a trainer with a secondary light attack capability. It is a license-built version of the Aermacchi MB-326GC. The manufacturer’s designation is EMB-326.

The AT-26 served with the Brazilian air force and, after 1982, with the Argentinian naval COAN.

The RT-26 is a reconnaissance version.

The AT-26A is a rebuilt ex-SAAF Impala Mk II. It was in service with the Brazilian FAB from 2004 to 2009.
