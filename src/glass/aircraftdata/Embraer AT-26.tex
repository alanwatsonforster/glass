\section{Embraer AT-26 Xavante}

The Embraer AT-26 Xavante is a trainer and light attack aircraft.

\subsection{Versions}

\subsubsection{AT-26}

The AT-26 is a two-seat trainer with a secondary light attack capability. It is a license-built version of the Aermacchi MB-326GC. The manufacturer’s designation is EMB-326. 

It served in the Brazilian FAB from 1971 to 2010 and also in the Argentinian naval COAN after 1982, making up for MB-326G aircraft lost in the South Atlantic War. 

\subsubsection{RT-26}

The RT-26 is a reconnaissance version of the AT-26

\subsubsection{AT-26A}

The AT-26A is a single-seat light attack aircraft. It is a rebuilt ex-SAAF Impala Mk II. 

If served in the Brazilian FAB from 2004 to 2009.

\subsection{Armament and Stores}

The AT-26 and RT-26 have no internal armament, but the AT-26A has two 30 mm DEFA cannons.

Air-to-ground stores options include 250-lb and 500-lb bombs, 7.62 mm gun pods, and rocket pods with seven, nineteen, or thirty-seven 70 mm rockets.
