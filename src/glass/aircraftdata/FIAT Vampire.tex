\section{FIAT Vampire}

The FIAT Vampire FB.52A is a day fighter and fighter-bomber. 

\subsection{Versions}

\subsubsection{FIAT Vampire FB.52A}

The FB.52A is a day fighter and fighter bomber. It is a license-built version of the corresponding de Havilland Vampire. Unusually, the FB.52A uses the Goblin 2 engine rather than the later and more powerful Goblin 3 engine used by many of the other fighter-bomber Vampires. 

It served in the Italian AMI, the Egyptian EAF, and the Syrian Air Force. FIAT built a total of one hundred and six aircraft for the Italian AMI with deliveries starting in December 1951. Some were subsequently sold on to Syria and Egypt.

\subsection{Armament and Stores}

The gun armament is four Hispano 20 mm cannons with 150 rounds per gun. 

A typical air-to-air load would be two 450L (100 imperial gallon) fuel tanks to increase endurance. 

A typical air-to-ground load would be eight RP-3 rockets and then either two 500 lb bombs or fuel tanks, depending on the mission radius. On short-range missions, two 1,000-lb bombs could be carried but without rockets.
