\aircrafttype{FIAT Vampire}

The FIAT Vampire FB.52A is a day fighter and fighter-bomber. It is a license-built version of the de Havilland Vampire FB.52A. 

FIAT built a total of one hundred and six aircraft for the Italian AMI with deliveries starting in December 1951. Some were subsequently sold on to Syria and Egypt.
