\groundunittype{Light Armor}

The \emph{light armor} ground unit represents a platoon of light armor. The \emph{light armor headquarters} ground unit represents a headquarters platoon of light armor.

Light armor includes APCs, IFVs, armored half-tracks, and other armored vehicles not specifically mentioned as heavy or medium armor or as one of the subsequent more specialized unit types. It may be tracked, wheeled, or half-tracked. The distinguishing characteristic is that if provides a degree of armored protection for the crew and load.
