\groundunittype{Light Armor}

The \emph{light armor} ground unit represents a platoon of light armor. The \emph{light armor headquarters} ground unit represents a headquarters platoon of light armor. There are also \emph{wheeled} and \emph{half-tracked} versions.

Light armor includes the 
AML,
AMX-10P,
AMX-13,
BMD-1/2/3/4,
BMP-1/2/3,
LVTP-7,
M2/3 Bradley,
M24 Chaffee,
M41 Walker Bulldog, 
M113,
M551 Sheridan, 
PT-76,
and Scorpion/Scimitar/Spartan.

Wheeled light armor includes the 
AMX-10 RC,
BTR-40/152/50/60/70,
BRDM-1/2/3,
EBR,
LAV,
and Stryker.

Half-tracked light armor includes the M3.
