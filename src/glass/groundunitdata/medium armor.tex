\groundunittype{Medium Armor}

The \emph{medium armor} ground unit represents a platoon of medium tanks.
The \emph{medium armor headquarters} ground unit represents a headquarters platoon of medium tanks.

Medium armor includes the AMX-30, Centurion, M4 Sherman, M47/48 Patton, M60, M103, Leopard 1, T-34, T-54/55, and T-62 tanks and exceptionally the Marder AFV.
