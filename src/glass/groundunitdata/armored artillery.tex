\groundunittype{Armored Artillery}

The \emph{armored artillery} unit represents a self-propelled artillery battery with light armor protection for the crew. There is also a \emph{multiplerocket} version.

Armored artillery includes the 
2S19 Msta-S, 
2S3 Akatsiya, 
AHS Krab, 
AMX-30 AuF1, 
AS-90,
FV433 Abbot, 
M43, 
M53, 
M55,
M108,
M109 Paladin, 
and Panzerhaubitze 2000.

Armored multiple-rocket artillery includes the M270 MLRS and TOS-1.
