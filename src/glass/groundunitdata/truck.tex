\groundunittype{Truck}

The \emph{truck} ground unit represents a platoon of trucks. There are \emph{light}, \emph{wheeled}, \emph{half-tracked}, and \emph{tracked} versions. The \emph{wheeled} version denotes a truck with all-terrain mobility rather than the default limited mobility.

Light trucks include Humvees, Jeeps, and Landrovers.

Wheeled trucks include the 
BAZ-6909,
HX,
LVSR,
MAZ-543/MAZ-7310 "Uragan",
and
HEMTT.

Tracked trucks include the AT-T and M548.
