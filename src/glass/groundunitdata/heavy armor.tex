\groundunittype{Heavy Armor}

The \emph{heavy armor} ground unit represents a platoon of heavy tanks. The \emph{heavy armor headquarters} ground unit represents a headquarters platoon of heavy tanks

Heavy armor includes the M1 Abrams, Conqueror, Chieftain, Challenger, Challenger II, IS-3, Leclerc, Leopard 2, Merkava, T-10, T-64, T-72, T-80, T-84, and T-90 tanks and exceptionally the Namer APC.
