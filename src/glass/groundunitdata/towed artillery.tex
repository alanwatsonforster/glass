\groundunittype{Towed Artillery}

The \emph{towed artillery} unit represents a towed artillery battery with guns such as the 
25-pdr,
105 mm L108 Light Gun, 
105 mm L118 Light Gun, 
105 mm M102, 
122 mm D-30, 
130 mm M-46,
152 mm D-20,
155 mm FH70, 
155 mm G5, 
155 mm M114, 
203 mm M115,
105 mm M119, 
155 mm M198, 
and
155 mm M777

It may be associated with a truck tractor unit.
