\groundunittype{Mobile Artillery}

The \emph{mobile artillery} unit represents a self-propelled artillery battery without complete armor protection for the crew and vehicle. There are also \emph{rocket} and \emph{missile} versions.

It may be wheeled, half-tracked, or tracked; the distinguishing characteristic is the lack of armored protection for the crew.

Mobile artillery includes
the 
2S4 Tyulpan,
2S7 Pion,
Archer,
CAESAR,
M7 Priest,
and
M107/M110.

Mobile rocket artillery includes the 
BM-21 Grad,
BM-30 Smerch,
and
M142 HIMARS.

Mobile missile artillery includes the 
9K52 Luna-M (Frog), 
MGM-52 Lance, 
OTR-21 Tochka (SS-21), 
Pluton,
and
