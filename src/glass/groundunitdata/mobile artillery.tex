\groundunittype{Mobile Artillery}

The \emph{mobile artillery} unit represents a self-propelled artillery battery without complete armor protection for the crew and vehicle. There are \emph{wheeled} and \emph{tracked} versions and also \emph{multiple-rocket} and {missile} version.

Tracked mobile artillery includes
the 
M7 Priest,
2S7 Pion,
2S4 Tyulpan
and
M107/M110.

Wheeled mobile artillery includes the Archer and CAESAR.

Wheeled multiple-rocket artillery includes the M142 HIMARS, BM-21 Grad, and BM-30 Smerch.

Wheeled missile artillery includes the 9K52 Luna-M (Frog), OTR-21 Tochka (SS-21), RSD-10 Pioneer (SS-20), 


Tracked missile artillery includes the MGM-52 Lance and Pluton.
