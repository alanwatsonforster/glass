## SA-3B

### Launch Unit

- From EOTG; I assume it is a SA-3B as SA-3s were supplied to Egypt in 1970.
- Values adopted from Puma
- Towed version seems to correspond to four quad launchers and four twin launchers in the mobile version.
- Puma has the mobile version correspond to only two launchers, which seems wrong. I have expanded this to four. He also has the 3A on fixed mounts and the 3B on mobile mounts, but I suspect the 3B is used on both.

### Missiles

- SA-3 was supplied to Egypt in 1970, so probably the SA-3B version. Support for this is that Puma has the SA-3A without OG.
- Values adopted from Puma, except that Puma gives a speed of 20-0 for CW mode and 14-2 for OG mode, which seems unlikely. I use 20 for both.

## SA-4

- Probably SA-4B 9M8M1, which was introduced in 1967.

### Launch Units

- From AS.
- Puma has a defense strength of 6H, which is difficult to understand. I use the 3H from AS.
- Puma seems to correspond to two dual armored TELs. AS has three TELs. Wikipedia suggests that 3 is correct.
- AS has no EWR. Puma has VF/50.
- Wikipedia suggests P-40 Long Track EWR at regimental level.

### Missile

- AS does not give sustainer.
- Adopt values from Puma.

## SA-6

# Launch Units

- From AS (B) and EOTG (A)
- Values taken from Puma.
- The defense strength of 6H seems high, even though it comes from AS. I will use 3H, like the SA-4 in AS.
- Wikipedia says the radar vehicle has an EWR 1S11 (Straight Flush) and an illuminater 1S31. The range of the EWR is 30 miles. However, it can be used with other EWRs with longer ranges, including Spoon Rest, Long Track, and Flat Face.
- Seems to be four triple TELs with one radar.
- Puma has a volley number of 3, but AS has 2. Wikipedia says only 1 or 2 missiles can be guided. I adopt 2.
- Wikipedia says the SA-6B is mounted on a new vehicle with the Fire Dome guidance radar.

### Missiles

- According to Wikipedia, the SA-4A is the 9M8/9M8M and the SA-4B is the 9M8M1/9M8M2. Puma assigns A/B to these latter two. I'm going to assume the 9M8 is similar to the 9M8M1

## SA-11

In AS

### Launcher

- AS has 4 ready missiles, so it seems to be a single vehicle. It does not have an EWR. However, the rules allow a SAM unit to lock-on with just its TTR (although an EWR gives a beneficial modifier).
- Puma gives 4 ready missiles, but also gives an EWR.
- Wikipedia states that a battery is two TELAR (four missiles each) and one TEL (6 missiles) and that a battalion has a command vehicle, and EWR, and three batteries.
- Wikipedia states that the 9K37 uses the 9S18 (Tube Arm) or 9S18M1 (Snow Drift) EWR.

### Missiles

- Puma has slightly different flight properties for the CW and OP versions. I have adopted the CW version.
