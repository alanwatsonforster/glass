\groundunittype{Infantry}

The \emph{infantry} ground unit represents an infantry platoon. The \emph{infantry headquarters} ground unit represents an infantry headquarters platoon.

%Each mechanized or motorized infantry unit is associated with an infantry unit. The mechanized or motorized unit represents the vehicles (infantry fighting vehicles, armored personnel carriers, or trucks) that transport the infantry unit. The infantry unit may be mounted or dismounted. 

%If mounted, it is not subject to sighting or attacks and does not count for stacking, but suffers the same damage as its associated vehicles and may not engage in barrage fire. 

%If dismounted, it is subject to the normal rules for sighting and attacks and may engage in barrage fire.

A mechanized infantry platoon will be associated with a platoon of APCs or IFVs, normally light armor but exceptionally medium or heavy armor. A motorized infantry platoon will be associated with a platoon of trucks.
