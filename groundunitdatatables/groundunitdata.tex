\twocolumn[\section{Generic Units}]

Generic units are provided to represent infantry, tanks and other AFVs, trucks, artillery, and air-defense CCU, EWR, and FCR systems. For artillery and air-defense units, we distinguish between armored, mobile (typically truck-born), and towed units.

Most infantry, tank, APC, IFV, and transport units are platoons. Most artillery, AAA, and SAM units represent batteries.

Second, a partial infantry symbol is used to denote the association of IFVs and APCs with infantry.

\subsection{Infantry}

Infantry are soldiers trained to fight on foot. The generic infantry units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{infantry}
\item \emph{infantry HQ}
\item \emph{infantry FAC}
\end{itemize}
The first two units are platoons. The \emph{infantry FAC} unit is an infantry forward air controller squad.

A mechanized infantry platoon will be associated with a platoon of APCs or IFVs. A motorized infantry platoon will be associated with a platoon of trucks.

\subsection{Tanks}

Tanks are AFVs specialized for direct-fire. The generic tank units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{heavy tank}
\item \emph{medium tank}
\item \emph{light tank}
\item \emph{heavy tank HQ}
\item \emph{medium tank HQ}
\item \emph{light tank HQ}
\end{itemize}
These are all platoons.

Heavy tanks include the M1 Abrams, Conqueror, Chieftain, Challenger 1 \& 2, IS-3, Leclerc, Leopard 2, Merkava, T-10, T-64, T-72, T-80, T-84, and T-90.

Medium tanks include the AMX-30, Centurion, M4 Sherman, M47/48 Patton, M60, M103, Leopard 1, T-34, T-54/55, and T-62. 

Light tanks include the AMX-10 RC, AMX-13, Ferret, M24 Chaffee, M41 Walker Bulldog, M551 Sheridan, Panhard EBR, PT-76, and Scorpion/Scimitar. 

Note that we include armored cars as tanks; we do not distinguish between wheeled, half-tracked, and tracked direct-fire vehicles.

\subsection{IFVs}

IFVs are AFVs carry infantry and provide fire support. The generic IFV units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{medium IFV}
\item \emph{light IFV}
\end{itemize}

These are both platoons. 

Medium IFVs include the CV90, M2A2 Bradley (but not other Bradley versions), and Marder. All other IFVs are considered to be light. There are currently no heavy IFVs.

There are no IFV HQ units; the headquarters platoon of a IFV-equipped mechanized infantry unit will consist of an infantry HQ platoon and a normal IFV platoon.

\subsection{APCs}

APCs are AFVs that carry infantry but do not provide significant fire support. The generic APC units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{heavy APC}
\item \emph{medium APC}
\item \emph{light APC}
\end{itemize}

All are platoons. 

Heavy and medium APCs are typically converted tanks. Heavy APCs include conversions of the
T-54/55 (Achzarit), Centurion (NagmaSho't, Nagmachon, Nakpadon, and Puma), and Merkava (Namer). Medium APCs include conversions of the Sherman (Kangaroo). 

Light APCs include the
AMX-10P,
BTR-152,
BTR-40,
BTR-50/60/70/80,
FV432,
M3 half-track,
M59,
M75,
M113,
M114,
Saracen,
Stryker,
and 
VAB.

There are no APC HQ units; the headquarters platoon of a APC-equipped mechanized infantry unit will consist of an infantry HQ platoon and a normal APC platoon. 

\groundunittype{Trucks}

Trucks are unarmored or only partially armored vehicles. The generic truck units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{truck}
\item \emph{light truck}
\end{itemize}

These are all platoons.

Trucks may be wheeled, half-tracked, or tracked; the distinguishing characteristic is the lack of armored protection for their crew and load. 

Light trucks include Humvees, Jeeps, and Land Rovers.

\groundunittype{Artillery}

Artillery is specialized for indirect fire. The generic artillery units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{towed artillery}
\item \emph{armored artillery}
\item \emph{mobile artillery}
\item \emph{armored rocket artillery}
\item \emph{mobile rocket artillery}
\item \emph{mobile missile artillery}
\end{itemize}

These are all batteries.

Towed artillery may be associated with a truck tractor unit.

Armored artillery includes the 
2S19 Msta-S, 
2S3 Akatsiya, 
AHS Krab, 
AMX-30 AuF1, 
AS-90,
FV433 Abbot, 
M43, 
M53, 
M55,
M108,
M109 Paladin, 
and Panzerhaubitze 2000.


Mobile artillery includes
the 
2S4 Tyulpan,
2S7 Pion,
Archer,
CAESAR,
M7 Priest,
and
M107/M110.

Armored rocket artillery includes the M270 MLRS and TOS-1.

Mobile rocket artillery includes the 
BM-21 Grad,
BM-30 Smerch,
and
M142 HIMARS.

Mobile missile artillery includes the 
9K52 Luna-M (Frog), 
MGM-52 Lance, 
OTR-21 Tochka (SS-21), 
Pluton,
and
RSD-10 Pioneer (SS-20).

\subsection{CCUs}

CCUs are air-defense command and control units. The generic CCU units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{mobile CCU}
\item \emph{armored CCU}
\end{itemize}

Mobile CCUs include the 9S737 Ranzhir.

Armored CCUs include the 9S80 PPRU-1.

\subsection{EWRs}

EWRs are air-defense early-warning radars. The generic EWR units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{towed EWR-A}
\item \emph{mobile EWR-A}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{FCRs}

FCRs are air-defense fire-control radars for towed AAA. The generic FCR units are:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{towed FCR-A/B/C/D}
\item \emph{mobile FCR-A/B/C/D}
\end{itemize}
