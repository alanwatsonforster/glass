# Vampire

## ADCs

## Notes and Changes

### Vampire F.3

- Goblin 2 and unclipped wings. 
- Goblin 2 (BAE, Watkins)
- Internal fuel 399 US gal = 130 fuel points (Goebel)
- Internal fuel 330 Imp gal = 390 US gallons (Watkins)
- Total fuel 530 Imp gal (Watkins) implies two 100 gal FTs
- External fuel tanks 455L or 909L (Goebel)
- External FTs 100/200 Imp gal (Watkins) = 450/900L
- No ejection seat (Goebel)

Used by RAF, RCAF, Norway (Wikipedia)

### Vampire FB.5

- Goblin 2 and clipped wings (Goebel, Wikipedia, Watkins)
- Armored protection around engine systems (Goebel, Watkins)
- No ejection seat (Goebel/Watkins)
- 100 imp gal FT (Watkins)
- Used by RAF, SAAF, Venezuela (Goebel)

### Vampire FB.6

- FB.5 with Goblin 3 (Goebel/Watkins)
- Goblin 3 (BAE)
- Built by de Havilland and under license by Emmen
- Used by Switzerland (Goebel/Watkins)

### Vampire FB.9

- FB.5 but with air conditioning (Goebel/Watkins) and new canopy to prevent misting (Watkins).
- No ejection seat (Goebel)
- Goblin 2 (Watkins)
- Version for Rhodesia had Goblin 3 (Goebel/Watkins)

### Sea Vampire F.20

- FB.5 with arrestor hook, strengthened undercarriage.
- I'm not sure why it is the F.20 and not the FB.20.

### Sea Vampire F.21

- Used for flexible deck experiments
- Strengthened undersurfaces

### Vampire F.30

- Nene engine (Watkins)
- Developed from F.2 (Watkins)
- Problems at high Mach because of additional intakes under fuselage (Watkins)
- CAC and Australia

### Vampire FB.50

- Export FB.6 (Goebel/Watkins)
- Sweden (Goebel/Watkins)
- Designated "J 28B" (Goebel/Watkins)

### Vampire FB.51

- FB.5 built under license by Sud-Est (Goebel)
- France (Goebel)

### Vampire FB.52

- Export version of FB.6 (Goebel/Watkins)
- 10 nations including India (Goebel)
- 51 delivered to Egypt from de Havillanf in 1949-1955 (Watkins)

### Vampire FB.52A

- Export FB.6 (Goebel), with some built under license by Fiat and Macchi
- Export FB.6 (Wikipedia).
- Goblin 2 engine (Watkins)
- Used by Italy and then sold to Egypt (Wikipedia).
- 45 former Italian AF reconditioned and delivered to Egypt in 1954 (Watkins)
- 13 former Italian AF reconditioned and delivered to Syria in 1954 (Watkins)
- 7 former Egyptian AF delivered to Jordan in 1956
- 13 former Egyptian AF delivered to Saudia Arabia in 1957

### Vampire T.55

### Engines

- Goblin 2: 
  - 3,100 lbf
  - F.3
  - FB.5/9/51 (Goebel)
  - Later Mk 1s (Wikipedia)
  - Perleberg's ADC has the F.3 with reduced maximum speeds and climb rates; I will presume these apply to the Goblin 2 version. The F.3 also has unclipped wing tips, which might reduce the speed at low altitude, but I can't see how they would reduce the rate of climb.
- Goblin 3: 
  - 3,350 lbf
  - 8% more thrust than the Goblin 2
  - FB.6/50/52/52A and FB.5 for Rhodesia (Goebel)
  - Watkins at one point says the 52A has the Goblin 2, but at another states that it has a Goblin 3 engine.
  - All from F.3 onwards (Wikipedia)
  - Perleberg's ADC has the F.5/9 with better maximum speeds and climb rates; I will presume these apply to the Goblin 3 version.
- Nene 102
  - 5,000 lb
  - Mk 53 (de Havilland) or SE.530 Mistral (Sud-Est)

### Ejection Seats

- No RAF Vampires had ejection seats (Goebel).
- Installed on Swiss FB.6 in 1960 (Watkins)

### Weapon Stations

Perleberg's ADC (and presumably the original Desert Falcons ADC) has the wrong weapon stations.

There are two outer wing stations that can carry FT and BB and four inner wing stations that can each carry two RP-3 RKs (Gobel; see also the photo of the FB.5 in Wikipedia).
